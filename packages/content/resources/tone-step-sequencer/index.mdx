# Tone.js Step Sequencer

A playable 16-step drum machine with **zero audio files**: every voice is synthesized at trigger time. The kick is a `MembraneSynth` pitch-dropping into the sub range, the snare and hat are shaped `NoiseSynth` hits (the hat through a highpass filter), and the bass is an `FMSynth` alternating two notes for groove.

Scheduling is the important part: the loop runs on `Tone.Transport.scheduleRepeat` in **audio-clock time**, so hits are sample-accurate regardless of frame rate, and UI updates ride `Tone.Draw` so the playhead paints in sync with what you hear.

## Highlights

- **Synthesized kit** — membrane kick, filtered-noise snare/hat, FM bass; tweakable, weightless, sample-free
- **Sample-accurate loop** — `scheduleRepeat("16n")` with the audio clock, never `setInterval`
- **Synced playhead** — visual updates scheduled through `Tone.Draw` at the exact hit time
- **Live tempo** — BPM slider writes straight to `Transport.bpm` while playing
- **Pattern tools** — comes loaded with a groove; clear it or restore it anytime
- **Autoplay-policy safe** — the AudioContext starts inside the Play click (`Tone.start()`)

## Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|-----------|
| Audio | Tone.js (Transport, MembraneSynth, NoiseSynth, FMSynth, Filter) |
| Grid | DOM buttons generated at runtime |
| Sync | Tone.Draw for visual scheduling |
| Typography | Chivo Mono + Inter |

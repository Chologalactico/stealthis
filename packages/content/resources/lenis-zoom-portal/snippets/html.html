Inside — 01

Scale is a promise.

The portal doesn't grow to an arbitrary size. It scales by exactly max(vw / w, vh / h), computed as a function-based tween value, so the frame lands flush with the viewport on every screen.

Inside — 02

Scrub, don't play.

Every beat — the scattering headline, the swelling frame, the welcome line — lives on one timeline tied to the scrollbar. Scroll back and the world politely reassembles itself.

Inside — 03

Weight comes from Lenis.

Native scroll jumps; Lenis glides. Its inertia is what makes the pinned zoom feel like a camera move instead of a slideshow.

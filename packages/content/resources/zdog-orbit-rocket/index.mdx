# Zdog Orbit Rocket

**Zdog** renders 3D models with round, flat-shaded strokes — the signature "designer toy" look. This scene composes an entire solar diorama from primitives: the planet is a single fat-stroked point, the ring is an `Ellipse`, the rocket is a `Cylinder` + `Cone` + three path-drawn fins, and every orbit is just an `Anchor` rotating its children.

## Highlights

- **Grab and spin** — `dragRotate: true` gives free-orbit control of the whole illustration; auto-rotation pauses while dragging
- **Nested orbits** — the rocket rides one rotating anchor, the moon counter-rotates on another; the rocket stays tangent to its path for free because children inherit anchor rotation
- **Flickering exhaust** — the flame cone's scale is modulated by a sine every frame
- **Pure primitives** — no models, no textures; ~20 shapes describe the entire scene
- **Reduced motion aware** — orbits freeze under `prefers-reduced-motion`, but drag-to-rotate keeps working with on-demand re-renders
